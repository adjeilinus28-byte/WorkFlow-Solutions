Under the hood

The parts you only notice when they’re missing

Every platform above runs on the same foundations. This is the part that decides whether software survives its second year.

01

Separated at the database

Multi-tenant with row-level security enforced in Postgres itself. Your records are scoped to your organisation at the storage layer — not merely filtered out of a screen.

02

Permissions that mean something

Roles map to permission domains with department scoping, down to per-person page visibility. A storeman sees the store. A director sees the board view.

03

History that can’t be edited away

Posted transactions can’t be quietly deleted. Corrections are recorded as reversals, so the trail an auditor asks for is still there a year later.

04

Alerts that reach a person

Threshold breaches and pending approvals send email immediately, with a scheduled digest for everything else. Nobody has to remember to open a dashboard.

05

Your data leaves when you want

Excel and PDF export on every register, matched to the same templates used for bulk import. Data moves both directions; nothing is held hostage.

06

Your name on it, not ours

White-labelled throughout — your organisation’s branding in the interface, on exports and on generated PDFs. We sign it quietly in the footer.

How we work

From first message to running system

Five steps, in this order. No stage of it requires a signature before you’ve seen the software working on your own data.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you run

    Sector, team size, number of sites, and what’s currently living in spreadsheets. A short message is enough.

  2. 02

    Demo on your data

    We walk you through the closest platform using a sample of your own records, so you’re judging it on your work, not ours.

  3. 03

    Trial for 30 days

    Your organisation set up as its own tenant, all features open. No card, no commitment, walk away at any point.

  4. 04

    Configure and onboard

    Roles, departments, branding and your existing records imported. We train the people who’ll use it daily.

  5. 05

    Run and support

    Updates roll out continuously. Support by email and WhatsApp, from the people who built the thing.
