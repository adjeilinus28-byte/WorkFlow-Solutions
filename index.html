Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about WorkFlow Solutions, our products, and how we work.

How does the 30-day free trial work?

You get full access to every feature of your chosen application for 30 days — completely free, no credit card required. At the end of the trial you can choose to pay the one-time fee, subscribe monthly, or walk away. No obligation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, mobile money (MTN, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo), and major cards. For institutional clients we can also arrange invoice-based billing. Contact us to discuss your preferred method.

How long does it take to get started?

Access is provisioned within 24 hours of your enquiry. There is no server setup, no installation, and no lengthy onboarding process. Your team can be working inside the platform the same day.

Can I switch between pricing plans?

Yes. If you start on a monthly subscription and later want to switch to a one-time license (or vice versa), contact us and we will adjust your account accordingly. We are flexible.

What industries do you serve?

Our software is purpose-built for institutions (government, healthcare, education), mining and extraction operations, and operational businesses (logistics, construction, retail, manufacturing). We also build custom solutions for any sector.

Is my organisation's data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share your data with third parties. You retain full ownership of all data you generate on the platform and can request an export at any time.

Do you offer custom software development?

Absolutely. If our off-the-shelf products do not fully fit your workflow, we design and build bespoke software from scratch. Submit a brief via our contact form and we will schedule a discovery call within 24 hours.

What support do you provide?

All paid plans include email support with a 24-hour response time. Priority support with faster response is included on one-time license plans. For custom development clients, we offer a dedicated point of contact throughout the project.
